## 1. Overview

At FRESHR, we stand behind our product. If FRESHR isn't the right fit for you, we offer a straightforward **7-day money-back guarantee** — no complicated hoops, no lengthy processes.

This policy applies to all paid subscription plans on FRESHR.

---

## 2. Eligibility for a Refund

You are eligible for a full refund if **all** of the following conditions are met:

- Your refund request is submitted **within 7 calendar days of the original purchase date**.
- Your account is in good standing (not suspended or terminated for a Terms of Service violation).
- You have not previously received a refund for a FRESHR subscription.
- The payment was made via **bKash** through ZiniPay.

---

## 3. What Is Covered

- Full refund of the subscription fee paid for the current billing period.
- Applies to **all subscription plans** (all tiers and durations offered at the time of purchase).

---

## 4. What Is Not Covered

Refunds will **not** be issued for:

- Requests submitted after the **7-calendar-day window** from the purchase date.
- Accounts that have been terminated due to a violation of our [Terms of Service](#).
- Partial refunds for unused portions of a subscription period beyond the 7-day window.
- Any transaction fees charged by ZiniPay or bKash (these are outside our control).

---

## 5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team by email:

📧 **team@freshr.cc**

Please include the following information in your email:

1. Your registered account name and email address
2. Your bKash transaction ID or ZiniPay payment reference
3. The date of purchase
4. A brief reason for your refund request *(optional but appreciated — it helps us improve)*

We aim to acknowledge your request within **2 business days**.

---

## 6. Refund Processing

Once your refund is approved:

- Refunds are returned to the **original bKash account** used for payment.
- Processing typically takes **3–7 business days** from the date of approval, depending on bKash processing times.
- You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

REFRSH is not responsible for delays caused by bKash or ZiniPay's processing timelines.

---

## 7. Account Access After Refund

Upon approval and processing of a refund:

- Your access to paid features will be **revoked immediately**.
- Your account will revert to the free tier (if applicable) or be deactivated.
- Any AI-generated content or exports completed prior to the refund remain yours to keep.

---

## 8. Disputes

If you believe your refund request was incorrectly denied, you may reply to our support email to escalate the issue. We are committed to resolving disputes fairly and promptly.

REFRSH reserves the final right to determine refund eligibility in accordance with this policy.
